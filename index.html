About Me

I am a sophomore at Nanjing University (Suzhou Campus), majoring in Intelligent Software and Engineering, focusing on High-Performance Computing (HPC) and low-level system development. My core strength is translating technical expertise into practical engineering outcomes.

Proficient in C/C++, with working knowledge of Java, Python, Go, HTML and JavaScript, I have in-depth mastery of the RISC-V RV64GC instruction set and common development toolchains (git, gcc, clang, gdb), enabling efficient work in Linux environments.

In HPC and GPU programming, I master CUDA and Triton, achieving 1200x optimization for GDN Decode and 50000x for GDN Prefill in FlashInfer AI Kernel Generation Contest (Track C).

As a core developer of UEMU and UEMU-NG RISC-V emulators, I built RV64GC subsets from scratch and led UEMU’s full-stack refactoring.
